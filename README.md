Privacy Policy
Stack Master: Billet Yard
Effective: March 17, 2026 Version 2.0 Mobile Game — iOS & Android
Plain-language summary: Stack Master itself collects nothing from you. However, the game displays ads served by Google AdMob, which may collect device and interaction data to deliver and measure advertisements. You can opt out of personalized ads at any time through your device settings.
01 Overview

BLT Innovations LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the mobile game Stack Master: Billet Yard (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains our data practices and those of our third-party advertising partner. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Stack Master is a single-player puzzle game. It does not require an account, login, or any personal details to play. We do not collect personal information directly. However, to support free access to the game, Stack Master displays advertisements served by Google AdMob, which may collect certain data as described in this policy.
02 Information We Collect Directly

BLT Innovations LLC does not directly collect, store, or transmit any personal information from users. No account creation or personal details are required to play Stack Master.

The following data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to us or any third party:

    Game progress and level completion status
    High scores and career score totals
    User preferences and settings (such as audio on/off)

03 Third-Party Advertising — Google AdMob

Stack Master displays ads served by Google AdMob, a mobile advertising platform operated by Google LLC. AdMob may collect and use certain data to serve, personalize, and measure advertisements, including:

    Device advertising identifiers — Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS, or Google's Advertising ID (GAID) on Android
    IP address — used to determine approximate (coarse) location, not precise GPS location
    Device information — device type, model, operating system version, screen size, and language settings
    Ad interaction data — impressions served, ads clicked, view duration, and frequency
    App usage signals — information about app activity used for ad targeting and measurement

All data collected by the AdMob SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. For full details on AdMob's data practices, please see:

    Google Privacy Policy
    AdMob & AdSense Program Policies
    Google Mobile Ads SDK Data Disclosure

App Tracking Transparency (iOS): On iOS 14.5 and later, Apple requires apps to request your permission before allowing ad networks to track you across other apps and websites. Stack Master will present this system prompt when you first launch the app. You may decline tracking — ads will still display, but they will be less personalized. You can change this setting at any time in your device's Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking.
04 Types of Ads Displayed

Stack Master may display one or more of the following ad formats:

    Interstitial ads — full-screen ads displayed between levels or game sessions
    Rewarded video ads — optional ads you may choose to watch in exchange for in-game benefits
    Banner ads — small persistent ads displayed at the edge of the screen

Rewarded ads are always optional and are never required to progress in the game.
05 Opting Out of Personalized Advertising

You have the right to opt out of personalized advertising at any time. Here is how:

    iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable tracking for Stack Master (or disable globally). You can also go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → turn off Personalized Ads.
    Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → select "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization."
    Google Ads Settings: Visit adssettings.google.com to manage ad personalization preferences across Google services.

Opting out does not eliminate ads — it means the ads you see may be less relevant to your interests.
06 No Other Third-Party SDKs

Outside of the Google AdMob advertising SDK, Stack Master does not integrate any analytics platforms, social media SDKs, crash reporting tools, or other third-party data collection services. We do not use Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, or any similar tools.
07 Children's Privacy

Stack Master is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Google AdMob may serve contextual (non-personalized) ads in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

If you believe a child under 13 has been improperly tracked through this App, please contact us using the information below, and we will take appropriate steps to address the issue.
08 Apple App Store Privacy Nutrition Label

In accordance with Apple App Store requirements, the privacy nutrition label for Stack Master discloses data collected by the Google AdMob SDK integrated in the App:

    Data Used to Track You — Device advertising identifiers and usage data may be used by AdMob to deliver targeted advertising across apps and websites (subject to your App Tracking Transparency permission on iOS)
    Data Linked to You — Ad interaction data may be linked to your device identifier by AdMob for advertising measurement
    Data Not Linked to You — Coarse location, device information, and diagnostics used for contextual ad serving

BLT Innovations LLC itself collects no data. All data collection described above is performed by the Google AdMob SDK.
09 Data Retention and Deletion

BLT Innovations LLC does not retain any user data on our servers, because we do not operate servers or collect data.

Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's data retention policies. You can manage or delete data Google holds about you at myaccount.google.com.

To clear all local game data (progress, scores, settings), simply uninstall the App from your device.
10 Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

    Access the personal data held about you
    Request correction or deletion of your data
    Opt out of the sale or sharing of your data
    Withdraw consent for data processing

Because BLT Innovations LLC collects no data, these rights apply primarily to data collected by Google through the AdMob SDK. You can exercise these rights through your Google Account settings or your device's privacy settings. You may also contact us and we will assist as best we can.
